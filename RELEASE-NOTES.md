Macro Tracker 1.5.7 makes food amounts and their nutrition basis clear when adding or editing foods.

- Adds an **Amount** field and a **Measure** menu with **Item / serving**, **Grams**, and **Ounces**.
- Keeps a natural description for countable foods such as `1 whole egg`, `1 slice`, `1 bottle`, or `1 scoop`.
- Uses fixed one-gram and one-ounce nutrition bases for weight entries, and updates every macro label and total preview to match.
- Recognizes gram and ounce portions returned by USDA search and reopens existing weight-based foods with the matching measure selected.

Update through Settings → App updates → Check for updates → Download update.
