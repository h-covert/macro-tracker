Macro Tracker 1.5.8 makes numerical food values faster to replace.

- Selects the entire existing value when you click into the **Amount**, calories, protein, carbs, or fat field in Add Food and Edit Food.
- Applies the same behavior when you reach those fields with Tab, so typing immediately replaces the old value.
- Retains the Item / serving, Grams, and Ounces measurement choices introduced in 1.5.7.

Update through Settings → App updates → Check for updates → Download update.
